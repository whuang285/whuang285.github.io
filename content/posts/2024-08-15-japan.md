| Hiroshima to Miyajima (round trip)      | ~$11.60 (train + ferry)    | Covered              | Covered                   |
| Hiroshima to Kyoto                      | ~$78                       | Covered              | Covered                   |

| Shin-Osaka to Himeji                    | ~$36                       | Covered              | Covered                   |
| Himeji to Kobe                          | ~$6.50                     | Covered              | Covered                   |

**Japan Travel: Kansai Hiroshima Pass & Shinkansen Ticket**

For a trip through Tokyo, Hiroshima, and the Kansai region (Kyoto, Osaka, and Kobe), the cheapest option is currently the **Kansai Hiroshima Pass** with a separate **Nozomi Shinkansen ticket**.

The **Kansai Hiroshima Pass** covers unlimited travel between Hiroshima and the Kansai region, perfect for getting around the local JR lines easily. To get from Tokyo to Hiroshima, purchase a separate **Nozomi Shinkansen ticket**, which offers the fastest route.

If you're planning a similar itinerary, you can get the **Kansai Hiroshima Pass** [here](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/3278-5-day-jr-kansai-hiroshima-area-pass-jr-pass/?spm=SearchResult.ShoppingCartBox_Activity_LIST&clickId=71e63c6dea) and the **Shinkansen ticket** [here](https://www.klook.com/rails-32/1012-japan/search/?date_range_count=89&origin_position_name=Tokyo&origin_position=99be1154-e69d-44cf-8fc4-8affe6e446a6&destination_position_name=Hiroshima&destination_position=ec6179f8-9b9d-45f6-b87c-b749a0ae2d35&departure_date=2024-10-08&passengers=%5B%5D&product_type=jprail).
</div>

{: .box-warning}
**Important Note:** When buying the Kansai Hiroshima Pass from overseas, you're actually purchasing a voucher called an **Exchange Order**. Upon arriving in Japan, you'll need to exchange this voucher at a majory station (including all international airports) to get the actual pass.
