**Planning my Second Japan Trip**

With its expansive rail network and various travel passes, it’s essential to choose the best option for your itinerary. Here’s a detailed cost comparison and travel guide for my upcoming journey covering Tokyo, Hiroshima, Kyoto, and Kansai.

## Planning

### Detailed Itinerary

#### Day 1 — Arrival in Tokyo

*Fly into Haneda Airport*

- **Time:** 4:50 PM
- **Activity:** Arrive at HND Airport.
- **Time:** Evening
- **Activity:** Dinner and Pokémon Café.

#### Day 2 — Tokyo

*Shopping in Tokyo, then evening bullet train to Hiroshima*

- **Time:** Breakfast
- **Activity:** Ramen at E-Chann (sells out by 10 AM).
- **Time:** Afternoon
- **Activity:** Visit Ueno Park and Ameyoko Shopping Street. Shopping at Nakano Broadway.
- **Time:** 6:00 PM
- **Activity:** Pick up suitcase from hotel and take the train to Hiroshima (~4 hours).
  - **Travel Option:** Nozomi Shinkansen
  - **Cost:** ~$123 (Klook)
  - **Note:** Not covered by JR Pass and Kansai-Hiroshima Area Pass

#### Day 3 — Hiroshima and Miyajima

*Visit Miyajima Island*

- **Time:** Morning
- **Activity:** Ferry to Miyajima Island. Watch the sunset on Miyajima. Return to Hiroshima for dinner.
  - **Travel Option:** JR Train + JR-West Miyajima Ferry
  - **Cost:** ~12

#### Day 4 — Hiroshima to Kyoto

*Explore Hiroshima (Peace Memorial Museum, Atomic Dome, Okonomiyaki), then travel to Kyoto*

- **Time:** Morning
- **Activity:** Visit Peace Memorial Park and Peace Memorial Museum. Pick up luggage from hotel and take the train to Kyoto (~1.5–2 hours). Arrive in Kyoto and check-in to accommodation before 8:30 PM.
- **Time:** 4:00 PM
- **Activity:** Train to Kyoto (~1.5–2 hours).
  - **Travel Option:** Hikari or Sakura Shinkansen
  - **Cost:** ~$78

#### Day 5 — Kyoto Exploration

*Explore Kyoto, optional visits to Fushimi Inari Taisha, Bamboo Forest, or a relaxing Onsen*

- **Time:** 8:00 AM
- **Activity:** Visit Fushimi Inari and Bamboo Forest.

#### Day 6 — Kyoto to Himeji to Kobe

*Day trip to Himeji Castle, then explore Kobe*

- **Time:** 8:00 AM
- **Activity:** Train to Shin-Osaka and store luggage at the station. Train to Himeji (~1 hour) to visit Himeji Castle.
  - **Travel Option:** JR Special Rapid Service
  - **Cost:** ~$40
- **Time:** 2:00 PM
- **Activity:** Head to Kobe and visit the Herb Gardens. Evening train to Osaka Station, pick up suitcase and transfer to KIX hotel.
  - **Travel Option:** JR Special Rapid Service and Shinkansen
  - **Cost:** ~$22.50

#### Day 7 — Departure from Kansai

*Early morning flight out of KIX*

- **Time:** 9:20 AM
- **Activity:** Flight out from Kansai.

> **Japan Travel: Kansai Hiroshima Pass & Shinkansen Ticket**
>
> For a trip through Tokyo, Hiroshima, and the Kansai region (Kyoto, Osaka, and Kobe), the cheapest option is currently the **Kansai Hiroshima Pass** with a separate **Nozomi Shinkansen ticket**.
>
> The **Kansai Hiroshima Pass** covers unlimited travel between Hiroshima and the Kansai region, perfect for getting around the local JR lines easily. To get from Tokyo to Hiroshima, purchase a separate **Nozomi Shinkansen ticket**, which offers the fastest route.
>
> If you're planning a similar itinerary, you can get the **Kansai Hiroshima Pass** [here](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/3278-5-day-jr-kansai-hiroshima-area-pass-jr-pass/?spm=SearchResult.ShoppingCartBox_Activity_LIST&clickId=71e63c6dea) and the **Shinkansen ticket** [here](https://www.klook.com/rails-32/1012-japan/search/?date_range_count=89&origin_position_name=Tokyo&origin_position=99be1154-e69d-44cf-8fc4-8affe6e446a6&destination_position_name=Hiroshima&destination_position=ec6179f8-9b9d-45f6-b87c-b749a0ae2d35&departure_date=2024-10-08&passengers=%5B%5D&product_type=jprail).

> **Important Note:** When buying the Kansai Hiroshima Pass from overseas, you're actually purchasing a voucher called an **Exchange Order**. Upon arriving in Japan, you'll need to exchange this voucher at a major station (including all international airports) to get the actual pass.
